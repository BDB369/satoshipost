Step by step

  1. Enter the from and to ZIP codes, box size and weight.
  2. Compare live USPS, UPS and FedEx rates and pick one.
  3. Enter the addresses. You get an order ID and a recovery key instead of an account. Save them.
  4. Pay the Bitcoin invoice from any wallet. Every order gets a fresh address.
  5. Once the payment confirms, download the label (PDF, 4x6 thermal, ZPL or EPL2), print it and drop the box off.

FAQ

Are these real carrier labels? Yes, with normal carrier tracking numbers.

Do I need an account for UPS or FedEx? No. All three carriers work without signing up.

Can I ship internationally? DHL Express covers international shipments; Canada Post works within Canada.
