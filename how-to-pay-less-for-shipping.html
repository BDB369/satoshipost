7 ways to pay less for shipping

  1. Skip the retail counter. Online labels are often far cheaper. In our Nov 19, 2026 quotes, a guitar was $48.25 online vs. $96.62 at the FedEx counter.
  2. Weigh the box at home. Guessing high wastes money; guessing low can get the package returned or charged extra.
  3. Use the smallest box that fits. Large, light boxes are billed by size (dimensional weight), not actual weight.
  4. Under 1 lb? Try USPS Ground Advantage. It's usually the cheapest US option for light packages. A small box from Arizona to California cost $8.30.
  5. Heavy box? Compare UPS and FedEx Ground. They often beat USPS once a package gets heavier.
  6. Compare all carriers at once. The cheapest carrier changes with weight and distance, so check every rate before buying.
  7. Ship often? Look at volume plans. SatoshiPost Pro ($19 for 30 days, paid in Bitcoin) lowers every label price.
